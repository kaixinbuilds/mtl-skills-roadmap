Planning · stock-take of current scheme-of-work documents · 31 October 2026

What the department plans with today

Every scheme-of-work (SOW) document found on the BVSS MTL shared drive, across Chinese, Malay and Tamil, with its format, how complete it is, and sample rows side by side. The point is not to grade anyone's document: it is to see which habits already work, so the redesigned SOW can be built from them.

1. Inventory

Documents on the drive whose purpose is to lay out teaching across weeks or terms. CPDD samples are included because the ML and TL units work from them; they are marked. Completeness = weeks with content ÷ weeks laid out in the document.

Not counted: assessment papers, lesson decks, the oral question frameworks, the reading programme planning document and the 40 Stories project plan. These are planning documents too, but for one component or event rather than a scheme of work. Teachers may keep SOWs on Google Drive, SLS or in personal folders that this scan could not see; the ML and TL rows below are therefore "not found on the shared drive", not "does not exist".

2. Six formats in use, across the three languages

Each card shows the document's columns and an excerpt of real rows (lightly trimmed). Hover is not needed; everything is in the table.

3. Within Chinese: one template, five variants

The CL unit shares one weekly template (周次 · 教学内容 · 重点任务 · 作业/练习 · 备注), but each level fills it differently. The variation is where the good practice hides.

A tick means the feature appears in the document itself. "Partly" means it appears in some weeks or only as a label. The 2026 S4 HCL document carries the heading 中三G3班级 on its Term 2 to 4 tables and repeats the S3G3 Term 1 content, so it reads as a copy that has not yet been adapted.

4. Across languages: which columns of the redesigned frame already exist where

The redesigned SOW has ten columns. This matrix shows which of them each language's current planning document already carries, so the template adds the fewest new habits for each unit.

5. Findings and what they mean for planning

How the redesigned SOW responds

  1. One frame, ten columns, each borrowed from an existing document (CL weekly pacing, ML learning outcomes and success criteria, TL period budget and MOE-initiative tags, the CL four-year plan's resources and adjustment columns).
  2. Pacing and purpose in one row. The CL weekly table and the CL yearly grid are merged, so a week shows what is taught, which CPDD learning point it serves, and which paper it builds toward.
  3. Two new columns for the 2027 school approaches: the E21CC priority focus with the student move, and the inquiry opportunity, filled from the tags on the roadmap and reviewed at unit meetings.
  4. Generated first draft for every course and year from the CPDD unit data, so no level starts from a blank table; the unit then edits adjustments, resources and success criteria.
  5. Three exemplars (CL 1G3, ML 1G3, TL 1G1) show the frame filled to lesson level, using the 2026 BVSS SOW content and the CPDD guides.

6. Method and caveats

  • Scanned: the department drive folders 02 Curriculum and Instruction Materials (CL, ML, TL), CPDD resources (2021 Syllabus), 01 Admin Matters and 05 Professional Development, for files named or structured as a scheme of work (SOW, 教学进度表, 教学总览, 教学计划, Skema Pengajaran, Rancangan, கண்ணோட்டம்).
  • Read: every table in each Word document; week rows were counted as filled when the 教学内容 or 重点任务 cell had text. Spreadsheet sheets were read in full.
  • Date: 31 October 2026, using file modification dates on the drive. Rerun tools/stocktake_gen.py against the drive to refresh the inventory, then tools/build_stocktake.py to rebuild this page.
  • Judgement calls: the format family and the feature ticks were assigned by reading the documents; they are recorded in this page's source so they can be corrected.
